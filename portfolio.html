What wood and flooring services do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of flooring services including hardwood installation, laminate, vinyl plank (LVP), carpet, and ceramic tile. Our expert team handles everything from subfloor preparation to final finishing touches.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and materials. Most residential projects take 3-7 days. We provide detailed timelines during the initial consultation and keep you updated throughout the installation process.

Can I share feedback during my project?

Absolutely! We encourage open communication throughout the project. Your feedback is important to us, and our team is happy to make adjustments to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final result.

What areas do you service?

The entire state of Georgia.
